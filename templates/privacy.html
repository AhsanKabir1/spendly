Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information.

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use the app, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so we can display them back to you.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core features of Spendly: authenticating your account, recording your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts to render the interface. Aside from this, Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
